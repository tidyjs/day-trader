# M4: ranking, provisional levels, sizing (2026-11-16)

Branch `m4-levels` (off `main`). Not committed. **Waiting on your review before M5.**

## What M4 does (`src/rank.py`)
- **Score (0-100)** over gap, prior-day volume multiple, catalyst quality and float. Weights are in `config.yaml` → `ranking` (gap 0.30, volume 0.30, catalyst 0.25, float 0.15), pre-declared, not tuned. An unknown float scores 0. Ties are broken by premarket dollar volume.
- **Provisional levels at 09:15** (the opening range doesn't exist yet; see SPEC 4.5):
  - trigger = premarket high + 1 tick
  - stop = 1 tick under the most recent premarket higher low (a pivot low above the pivot low before it), or else under the lowest low of the last 30 premarket minutes
  - 1.5R target
  - skip if the stop is more than 12% below the trigger
- **Sizing per risk profile:** `measure` (5% risk, position capped at equity, fractional shares floored to 0.01) and `challenge` (blank, so "profile not set").
- **Shortlist:** Tier A first, then Tier B, by score, at most 5. Names whose levels are a skip get no rank.
- **Levels are held back from the live message** until you've reviewed them. The message shows tiers, scores and the "data up to about 09:00 ET, re-check the live premarket high" note.

## Findings for your review (nothing changed for these)
1. **The trigger is often far above the current price.** SPEC 4.5's trigger is the premarket *high*. When that high came from an early spike, the trigger sits hours-old and far above the price, and the higher-low stop is too wide, so the name is a skip:
   - CRMN 11-12: high 10.84 at 04:41 ET, a real spike (277,854 shares in 6,247 trades), against a 08:58 price of 1.71
   - SLSN 11-13: high 1.35 at 04:02, against 1.15
   - ICMB 11-12: high 1.16 at 07:09, against 1.04

   **Both Tier A names are skips.** Options (your call; none implemented):
   - (a) keep SPEC as written; such names rarely trigger, and M6 will show it
   - (b) use the high of the last 60 premarket minutes
   - (c) leave levels alone, but list such names as "far below premarket high" instead of a level
2. **Tier B includes names over the float cap** (KLTR 92M, ZNTL 78M, GEVO 228M), because your definition doesn't list float. Keep it, or add float to Tier B?
3. **At $50 equity with tight stops, the position cap usually binds** (KLTR, ZNTL, GEVO), so the real risk is $1.77-2.30, below the 5% budget of $2.50.
4. **Under the effective-date split rule, no name is disqualified for a reverse split any more.** YMAT's new 1-for-10 and AMIX's authorised split have no effective date, so they're "possible" warnings. AMIX is now Tier B (and a skip on levels). EZGO's split is completed (Nov 5, a warning).
5. **The 3-run classifier changed no disqualifier verdict** on the five days. Cost: 87 calls, about $0.0037 per day.
